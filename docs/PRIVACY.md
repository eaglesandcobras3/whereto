# Privacy — user feedback

**WhereTo30A** collects **private, internal-only** feedback when users report a bad fit, a bad experience, inaccurate info, or “don’t show again.” That data is used to improve ranking and quality; it is **not** shown publicly on business pages or to other end users.

Operators should:

- Restrict access to `user_feedback`, `user_suppressions`, and admin tools to trusted staff.
- Follow your retention policy for raw feedback rows (delete or aggregate on a schedule if required by law).

This is not legal advice; add your own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for your deployment.
