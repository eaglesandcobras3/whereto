# Hotz Coffee

Located just steps away from the iconic Red Bar and the main beach access, Hotz Coffee is Grayton Beach’s neighborhood refueling station. Whether you’re a local heading to work or a visitor grabbing a cup before hitting the sand, it’s the perfect embodiment of Grayton’s "laid-back morning" vibe.

## The Vibe

Hotz Coffee is unpretentious and cozy. It feels like a place where everyone knows each other, with local art on the walls and a steady flow of conversation. The outdoor seating is a great spot to people-watch as the town wakes up and the beach-goers start to arrive.

## What to Order

- **The Espresso:** Consistently well-pulled and smooth.
- **Cold Brew:** A local staple during the hot Florida summers.
- **Breakfast Sandwiches:** Quick, fresh, and perfect for taking to the beach.
- **Pastries:** Rotating selection of locally-made treats.

## Why We Love It

It’s one of those essential pieces of the Grayton Beach ecosystem. It doesn’t try to be anything other than a great local coffee shop, and in a coast filled with high-concept dining, that simplicity is refreshing. 

**Local Tip:** Grab your coffee and take a short walk down Hotz Avenue to the beach to enjoy the morning stillness before the crowds arrive.
