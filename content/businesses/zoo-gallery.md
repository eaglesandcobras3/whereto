# The Zoo Gallery

If Grayton Beach had a front porch for its artistic soul, it would be The Zoo Gallery. Since opening its doors in 1979, this eclectic space has been a cornerstone of the 30A art scene, showcasing a collection that is as vibrant and unconventional as the town itself.

## The Collection

Walking into The Zoo Gallery is an sensory experience. It’s not your typical, hushed "white walls" gallery. It’s packed with:
- **Hand-crafted Jewelry:** Unique pieces from artists around the country.
- **Eclectic Art:** Everything from whimsical pottery to large-scale paintings.
- **Home Decor:** Items that bring a bit of Grayton’s funky spirit into your house.
- **Apparel & Accessories:** Carefully curated finds that you won't see in the big-box stores.

## The History

The Zoo Gallery was one of the first businesses to establish itself in Grayton Beach, and it has played a huge role in maintaining the town’s reputation as an artistic haven. It’s still family-owned and operated, and that personal touch is evident in every corner of the shop.

## Why We Love It

It’s the perfect place to find a gift that actually feels like it came from the beach—not just a generic souvenir. Every item has a story, and the staff is always happy to share information about the artists they represent.

**Local Tip:** Even if you’re not looking to buy, it’s worth a visit just to see the incredible variety of work on display. It’s like a mini-museum of coastal creativity.
