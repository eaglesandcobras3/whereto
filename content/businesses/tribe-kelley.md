# Tribe Kelley Surf Post

Housed in a charming historic cottage in the heart of Grayton Beach, Tribe Kelley Surf Post is more than just a clothing store—it’s a reflection of the modern, bohemian spirit that has taken root along 30A. 

## The Brand

Founded by Brittany and Brian Kelley, the brand emphasizes quality and ethical manufacturing. Walking into the "Surf Post," you'll find a carefully curated selection of American-made apparel that feels perfectly suited for life on the Emerald Coast. The designs are comfortable, stylish, and built to last.

## The Experience

The store itself is beautiful, preserving the original architecture of the historic Grayton house while filling it with light, modern fixtures, and a rotating collection of curated goods. You’ll find everything from their signature "Butter" sweats to unique jewelry, home goods, and beach essentials.

## Why We Love It

It’s a great example of how Grayton Beach is evolving—honoring its historic past while introducing fresh, high-quality concepts. Whether you're looking for a specific piece or just browsing, the atmosphere is welcoming and distinctively coastal. 

**Local Tip:** Check out their outdoor space, which often hosts community events and pop-ups during the busier months.
