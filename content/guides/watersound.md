## Planning a WaterSound week honestly

I assume I will leave the neighborhood for most “big” dining nights. I pick anchors a few days out, and I protect slow mornings so the trip does not become a corridor marathon.

## Before you go

- Book weekend reservations toward central 30A early
- Confirm beach access and any community-specific rules for guests
- Decide on bikes early if you are imagining pedal dinners in summer heat
- Re-check hours the week you arrive

## Food worth planning

[[the-big-chill-watersound]] can be a simple win when I want something casual without overthinking the night. For special meals, I look east and accept drive time as part of the plan.

## Beach days and getting around

Cluster your trips. One grocery run, one eastbound dinner, one westbound lunch beats six scattered dashes.

## If you are here with kids

Keep a no-drive dinner option in your pocket for the tired nights. Those nights will happen.

## If you are here without kids

Pick one reservation worth the drive, then give yourself a full slow morning after. Quiet stays work best with intentional recovery time.
