## Planning a Prominence week

I decide how many nights we are keeping local versus driving east for a different scene. If I do not decide, I end up in accidental loops along 30A.

## Before you go

- Skim the current Hub schedule. Lineups change by season
- Book farther-east dinners early if you want prime weekend slots
- Confirm pool and amenity rules for your rental
- Re-check hours for any specific venue you are building a night around

## Food and evenings

I use The Hub for easy group energy, then I plan one reservation night elsewhere on the corridor when I want a change of pace.

## Beach days and getting around

Pad time whenever 30A is in the route. Short miles are not always short minutes.

## If you are here with kids

Keep one no-drive dinner option. That night will come up.

## If you are here without kids

Do one Hub night with no agenda after. Let it run long if it wants to.
