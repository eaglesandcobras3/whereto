## Planning a Miramar Beach week

I pick whether this trip is “mostly here” or “here plus east.” If I do not choose, I spend half the week debating drives at 5 p.m.

## Before you go

- Confirm beach access for your specific rental or resort
- Book a few dinners if you care about prime times in peak weeks
- If you are outlet shopping, plan it as a half-day, not a pit stop before dinner
- Re-check hours for seasonal venues

## Food and evenings

I rotate casual beach-adjacent nights with one destination night elsewhere. Miramar holds its own for easy wins, but variety is a short drive away.

## Beach days and getting around

Assume parking friction on perfect-weather afternoons. Earlier beach setup beats later heroics.

## If you are here with kids

Keep pool time as a real backup when the beach is rough or everyone is tired.

## If you are here without kids

Pick one slow shopping or spa block, then one night out without a tight return time.
