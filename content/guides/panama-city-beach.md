## Planning a PCB trip honestly

I pick a zone that matches my group. The strip is long enough that “Panama City Beach” on a listing is not enough detail. Then I build a loose plan: beach mornings, one attraction block, one late night if we want it.

## Before you go

- Read flag conditions and local beach safety guidance
- Book attractions in peak weeks if you care about time slots
- Map where you are staying relative to dinner and music spots
- Re-check hours and seasonal closures

## Beach days and traffic

Front-road traffic spikes on perfect afternoons and event weekends. I try to move earlier or later, and I avoid stacking long drives with a tired crew.

## Food and evenings

Mix one classic tourist boardwalk energy night with one quieter dinner away from the busiest strip if you need a break.

## If you are here with kids

Build breaks. Sun and stimulation add up faster than the itinerary suggests.

## If you are here without kids

Pick one live-music night, then one slow morning. The trip feels better with contrast.
