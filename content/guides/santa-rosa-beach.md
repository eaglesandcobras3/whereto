## Read the pin, not just the zip

Santa Rosa Beach is a planning category. Before I get excited about “close to everything,” I map the actual drive to beach access and to the restaurants I want. That single step prevents a lot of group frustration.

## Before you go

- Confirm beach access rules for your specific community
- Book prime dinners if you are aiming toward Seaside or Rosemary on weekend nights
- Decide whether you need chairs and umbrellas delivered, especially for bigger groups
- Re-check hours and closures the week you arrive

## Food worth planning

I rarely try to “wing” central 30A on a Saturday without a plan. I pick anchors a few days out, then keep flexible nights for whatever is easy near the house.

## Beach days and getting around

Pack the car once when you can. Multiple back-and-forth trips across 30A can eat a half day faster than it sounds.

## If you are here with kids

Keep one dinner that is intentionally close to your rental. A long drive home after sand and sun is where trips go sideways.

## If you are here without kids

Pick one corridor night worth the traffic, then give yourself a slower morning after. The week feels better with one recovery beat.
