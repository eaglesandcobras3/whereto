## Planning a Sandestin stay

I decide how many nights stay local at Baytowne versus driving out. If every night tries to be “somewhere else,” the resort convenience disappears.

## Before you go

- Read your stay’s beach access and shuttle details carefully
- Book golf if you care about tee times in peak weeks
- Check the village calendar for festivals and crowds
- Re-check hours for restaurants you care about

## Baytowne vs beach days

I do not stack a full beach day, a golf morning, and a late village night without a real break. Pick two, then let the third be flexible.

## Food and evenings

Mix one lively Baytowne night with one quieter night at home. Sandestin is built for that rhythm.

## If you are here with kids

Use the paths and shorter loops. Little legs tire fast when everything looks close on the map.

## If you are here without kids

Do one slow marina or sunset walk, then let dinner run late without a hard return plan.
