## Visiting Grayton with your shoes sandy

I plan fewer “fancy” nights and more room for spontaneity. I still reserve when it matters, because iconic spots fill up, but the vibe here is supposed to feel human, not optimized.

## Before you go

- Check dinner wait realities on weekend nights. Assume peak season friction
- Read parking notes before you promise a reservation time you cannot reach
- Confirm beach access for your rental if you are not walking from the sand
- Re-check hours the week you arrive

## Where we eat

- [[chiringo-grayton-beach]] for casual food and easy group energy
- [[red-bar]] when we want the Grayton night everyone argues about in the best way

If the wait looks wild, I pivot early instead of starving the group in the parking shuffle.

## Beach days and getting around

Pad time before reservations. Grayton is small, but access and parking do not always behave like the distance on the map.

## If you are here with kids

Keep snacks and a backup dinner plan. A hungry kid plus a long wait is the fastest way to ruin a beach day.

## If you are here without kids

Let one night run late. Grayton is built for music drifting into the evening when you stop watching the clock.
