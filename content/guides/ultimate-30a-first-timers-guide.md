## The guide I wish I had before my first 30A trip

The first time I planned a 30A trip, I opened a dozen tabs and still felt lost. I had two kids asking about the pool, my husband asking where he could paddleboard, and me trying to figure out if I should book Rosemary, Seaside, or somewhere I had never heard of. If that sounds familiar, this guide is for you.

30A is not one single beach town. It is a stretch of communities with completely different personalities. Once you pick the right town for your trip style, everything gets easier.

## What 30A actually is

30A is Scenic Highway 30A in South Walton County, Florida. It runs along the coast between the Panama City Beach side and the Santa Rosa Beach side. People call it a highway, but most of it feels more like a beach road. It is slow, walkable in many spots, and full of bikes and golf carts.

The water is why people keep coming back. It is usually clear, bright, and that unreal turquoise everyone posts. The sand is soft and white, and yes, it really does look that way in person.

## How to pick where to stay

For first-timers, the hardest part is picking a base. You cannot just search "30A" everywhere and call it done. You need to pick a town and then search by that town.

Most trips in the summer are planned well in advance. If you are targeting June or July, start early. Also note that many homes are weekly rentals with Saturday check-in and check-out.

### Simple town matching

- Inlet Beach: practical base with access to east 30A
- Rosemary Beach: walkable, polished, and very design-forward
- Seacrest: family-friendly and close to everything on the east side
- Alys Beach: architectural and upscale
- Watersound: quiet and more private
- Seagrove Beach: central access with solid dining options
- Seaside: iconic and very family-focused
- WaterColor: resort-style and easy for families
- Grayton Beach: laid-back, artsy, and local-feeling
- Blue Mountain Beach: casual and low-key
- Santa Rosa Beach: broad area with great practical options
- Dune Allen Beach: western edge, quieter pace

## Family notes and adult-only notes

With kids, I prioritize walkability, quick food options, and easy beach transitions. With a girls trip, I care more about dinner pacing, wine lists, and where we can sit outside and actually hear each other. 30A can do both, but the town you pick really shapes your day.

## Final thoughts

If you are stuck choosing, pick one side and commit. You can always explore the rest by bike or by short drives. Your first trip should be about getting a feel for the rhythm, not trying to do every single town in one weekend.
