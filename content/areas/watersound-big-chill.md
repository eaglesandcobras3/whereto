## The Big Chill at WaterSound

The Big Chill is a modern food-hall style hub with outdoor seating, stalls, and enough variety that picky eaters stop arguing. It is one of the easier “everyone picks their own thing” nights on the corridor.

## Main draws

Rotating food concepts, casual seating, and room to let kids move without hovering at one restaurant table all night. Small shops and services fill in around the edges depending on the season.

## Who it fits

Families and groups who want flexibility without a formal reservation night. Neighbors in WaterSound and Seagrove often treat it as the local weeknight answer.
