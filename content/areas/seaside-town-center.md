## Seaside Town Center

Seaside’s center is the postcard people picture: pastel cottages framing a tight loop of shops, food windows, and the amphitheater lawn. It is built for wandering, not for rushing.

## Main draws

Long-running restaurants, market-style shopping, sweets, books, and beach gear. When events are on the lawn, the whole square changes tempo.

## Who it fits

First-timers who want the iconic 30A town experience, and families who can handle crowds in peak weeks if the payoff is atmosphere. I go early or late if I want the same streets with less friction.
