## Grand Boulevard

Grand Boulevard is Sandestin’s main-street outdoor center. It is the opposite of a single beach hamlet square: wider sidewalks, bigger parking fields, and a directory that mixes national retail with local restaurants.

## Main draws

Movies, apparel, home goods, and enough dining variety to cover date nights and family meals. I use it when I need something I cannot find in a 30A boutique, or when rain threatens and I want an indoor backup near dinner.

## Who it fits

Anyone splitting time between Destin, Sandestin, and Scenic 30A. If you only have one week and never leave the beach towns, you might skip it. If you live like locals mix errands with beach days, you will end up here eventually.
