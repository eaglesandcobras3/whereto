## Pier Park

Pier Park is one of the biggest open-air retail and entertainment districts on the Gulf Coast in this region. It mixes big national anchors with beach shops, restaurants, and the energy of Panama City Beach’s tourist core.

## Main draws

Broad shopping for clothes, gear, and souvenirs, plus dining for every budget and a pier-adjacent beach-town atmosphere. It is built for volume, not intimacy.

## Who it fits

Travelers who want one-stop errands, rainy-day distractions, or a louder night out than a small 30A square offers. If you are staying strictly mid-30A, it is a drive, so I only make the trip when the errand list justifies it.
