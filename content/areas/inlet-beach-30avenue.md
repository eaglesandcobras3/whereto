## 30Avenue

30Avenue is a planned outdoor retail row on the Inlet Beach side of the corridor. It reads less like a historic square and more like a clean, easy loop: park once, walk a strip of restaurants and shops, and get back to the beach without a long drive.

## Main draws

Dining anchors get most of the traffic, from breakfast lines to casual Mexican and Italian nights. You will also find apparel, gifts, and beach basics mixed in with services visitors actually need mid-trip.

## Who it fits

Families who want predictable parking and a few strong meal options without navigating a full town center. Anyone staying east on 30A who wants errands and dinner in one stop.
