The Seaside Farmers Market returns to Central Square every Saturday morning from 9am to 1pm throughout May. Browse local produce, artisanal cheeses, fresh-baked goods, and handcrafted items from coastal Florida vendors.

## What to Expect

- Fresh local produce and seafood
- Artisan breads and pastries
- Live acoustic music
- Family-friendly activities

## Parking

Free parking available in the main Seaside lots. Arrive early for the best selection!
