# Grayton Beach State Park

While most visitors flock to the historic district of Grayton Beach, the real heart of the community lies within Grayton Beach State Park. Spanning nearly 2,000 acres, it offers a glimpse into what the Emerald Coast looked like before development—a landscape of massive dunes, wind-swept oaks, and rare coastal dune lakes.

## The Beach

Consistently ranked as one of the best beaches in the United States, the park's coastline is pristine. Unlike the crowded accesses in town, you'll find plenty of space to spread out here. The sand is that famous "sugar white" quartz, and the water is a brilliant emerald green.

## Western Lake

One of the park's most unique features is Western Lake, a 100-acre coastal dune lake. These lakes are extremely rare, found only in a few places worldwide (like Australia and Madagascar). 

**Activities on the Lake:**
- **Paddleboarding & Kayaking:** Rent a board at the park office and explore the calm waters.
- **Fishing:** Both saltwater and freshwater fishing are possible here.
- **Photography:** The "Ghost Trees"—dead pines standing in the water—make for some of the most iconic photos on 30A.

## The Trails

The park features several miles of trails that wind through various ecosystems:
- **Coastal Forest:** Walk through towering pines and scrub oaks.
- **Dune System:** See some of the highest dunes on the coast.
- **Bird Watching:** Keep an eye out for bald eagles, ospreys, and various migratory birds.

## Camping and Cabins

For those who want to stay immersed in nature, the park offers:
- **Full-facility campsites:** For both tents and RVs.
- **Modern Cabins:** Tucked away in the pine forest, these offer a more comfortable way to "rough it."

## Local Tips for the Park

1. **Get there early:** During peak season, the park can reach capacity by mid-morning.
2. **Bring Water:** Once you're out on the trails or the beach, facilities are far apart.
3. **Sunsets:** The park is one of the best places on 30A to watch the sun dip below the horizon without buildings in your view.
4. **Entrance Fee:** Expect to pay a small fee per vehicle, which goes directly to maintaining the park’s beauty.
